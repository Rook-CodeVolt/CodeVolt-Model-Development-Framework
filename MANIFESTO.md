# The CodeVolt Model Evolution Manifesto

Models should not change because a training job completed. They should change because a bounded, reproducible experiment produced independently verifiable evidence that the candidate is better for its declared purpose and has not crossed its safety or regression limits.

CodeVolt is building an open framework in which model development is understandable, extensible, and accountable. We will learn from strong existing projects, integrate them through explicit contracts, contribute upstream where appropriate, and grow distinctive CodeVolt capabilities through evidence rather than rebranding.

We value:

- verified improvement over impressive-looking output;
- provenance over opacity;
- modularity over a difficult-to-maintain mega-fork;
- reversible experiments over irreversible automation;
- recorded failures over selectively published success;
- human accountability over claims of autonomous authority;
- a welcoming contributor community over closed invention.

Our long-term destination is a coherent CodeVolt toolset for developing, training, evaluating, and evolving models. We will reach it progressively: first own the contracts and assurance layer, then prove integrations, then internalise capabilities only where doing so creates clear technical and community value.
