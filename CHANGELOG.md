- ADR-0020 (`docs/decisions/ADR-0020-decode-sensitive-refusal-eval.md`): preregisters a decode-sensitive, non-greedy-sampling evaluation of the existing ADR-0018 DPO candidate against its reference/base checkpoint, following the owner decision (Rook) to pursue Maya's decode-sensitive-scoring recommendation from ADR-0019's outcome record over a larger refusal-only held-out set. - ADR-0019's real gated evaluation ran to completion (`adr0019-logprob-margin-eval-20260923`, result file sha256 `90821432f08e68cfb08958fd298667e1d78ec53132748f2a3a36f6906b3197b9`): a preregistered held-out log-probability margin measurement of the ADR-0018 DPO candidate against its immutable base/reference checkpoint. On the authoritative full 20-pair held-out set, every pair's candidate-minus-reference margin moved in the trained direction (Wilcoxon `p = 9.6e-5`, statistic at its maximum possible value), with a Hodges-Lehmann point estimate (`0.679`) and 95% bootstrap CI (`[0.429, 0.787]`) landing entirely inside the preregistered `theta = 0.843` threshold — the preregistered outcome-3 classification, "no shift of training-set magnitude," fires exactly as defined, unrelabeled. That same outcome's own preregistered text leaves open a smaller real shift below `theta`, and this record reports, as factual observation rather than reclassification, that the point estimate sits at roughly 81% of `theta` with all 20 pairs positively signed — a real, sub-threshold, held-out probability-space shift that ADR-0018's decode-level (greedy-argmax) evaluation could not have surfaced either way. Secondary, non-authoritative subset breakdowns: the 16-pair refusal-direction subset's CI upper bound exceeds `theta` by about 0.001 (classified `ambiguous_underpowered`, flagged as not to be over-read given how far inside the bootstrap's own noise floor that margin sits); the 4-pair counter-direction subset shows a much smaller, non-significant shift (`no_shift_of_training_set_magnitude`, Wilcoxon `p = 0.100`). No length-normalization divergence was found. `docs/decisions/ADR-0019-outcome.md` maps this result against ADR-0019's own section 4 outcome-3 row and states, with every explanation labeled a hypothesis rather than a finding, that the result weakens the "training package too small to generalise at any magnitude" hypothesis and strengthens the "log-probability shift without a decode-level flip" hypothesis, while leaving the "conservative beta/learning-rate" hypothesis least directly addressed. It does not reopen or soften ADR-0018's own negative decode-level verdict. `examples/metatrainer-corpus-addition-adr0019-subtheta-heldout-shift-lesson/` adds this result as new draft corpus case-study material (not yet independently audited or admitted to any training-eligible corpus), teaching the reasoning pattern that a decode-level null and a separately measured, real, sub-threshold probability-space positive result are answers to two differently sensitive questions about the same model and are not in contradiction. This record does not propose or authorize a next training run or scoring-method change; a recommendation is named only as a recommendation for whoever holds that decision next, requiring its own fresh ADR.
